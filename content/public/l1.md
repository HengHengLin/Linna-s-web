## 金融词汇

- Underlying asset — 底层资产
- Enforceable rights — 可执行的权利
- Custody — 托管
- Redemption — 赎回
- Settlement — 交收

## 地道表达

> The key question is whether token holders have enforceable rights to the underlying assets.

> From a regulatory perspective, we need to clarify the ownership structure.
